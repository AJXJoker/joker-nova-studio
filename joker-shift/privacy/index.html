Joker Shift Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

Joker Nova Studio develops Joker Shift. Joker Shift does not require an account and does not send your shifts, work logs, pay settings or estimated earnings to Joker Nova Studio.

The app stores your schedule, work logs and settings on your device. Android automatic cloud backup is disabled for the app. You can create your own backup using JSON export. If you enable shift reminders, Android can show upcoming shift details in notifications. You can disable reminders in the app or change notification visibility in Android settings.

When you export CSV or JSON, you choose where Android saves the file. A storage provider you select may sync it under its own terms. Exported files can contain work and pay details. To remove local app data, clear Joker Shift’s storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Exported copies must be removed separately from the location where you saved them.

Joker Shift does not use advertising or analytics SDKs. For privacy questions, contact Joker Nova Studio at joker.1107410837@gmail.com.
